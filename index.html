Guardrails this agent follows

  • Business-scoped AI only (hiring, payroll, booking), in line with Meta's 2026 WhatsApp Business AI terms.
  • Says it's an AI and offers a person on every screen.
  • Consent logged with source, timestamp and scope before any marketing message.
  • Internal cap: max 2 marketing messages per contact per month, on top of Meta's own limits.
  • No marketing templates to +1 numbers while Meta's US pause is in place.
  • STOP works everywhere: one opt-out suppresses WhatsApp and marketing email.
  • No IDs, bank details or salaries of named people collected in chat.
  • Playful voice, serious about money: no jokes in payroll, tax or contract messages.

What each journey reports

  • Opt-in rate by entry point and market
  • Reply rate and qualified rate per template
  • Meetings booked and pipeline per 1,000 conversations
  • Cost per qualified conversation (Meta + BSP fees)
  • Block, report and opt-out rate (quality guardrail)
  • Lift vs email-only holdout group
Costs use Meta's list rates per delivered message effective 1 Oct 2026 (UK utility $0.0220, UK marketing $0.0635; messages inside a click-to-WhatsApp 72-hour free entry window cost $0).
