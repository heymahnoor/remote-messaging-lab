How the score works

  • Reach: share of people using WhatsApp (or messaging apps), scaled 0–1.
  • Cost efficiency: cheaper marketing messages score higher (Meta list rates, Oct 2026).
  • Low friction: my 1–5 read of consent and data rules (5 = hardest). Legal should own the final call.
  • Pipeline share: where Remote's buyers and hires actually are. The most important input, and the one only Remote has.
  • Gated markets (like +1 numbers, where Meta has paused marketing templates) are shown but not ranked: utility and service messages only.

Sources

  • WhatsApp reach by country: Infobip, WhatsApp statistics 2026
  • Germany, Netherlands (any messaging app, Q1 2025): Destatis / Eurostat
  • Marketing rates (1 Oct 2026): Meta rate card via Kanal, EngageLab
  • US marketing pause, frequency caps: Chatarmin, WhatsApp limits (Sept 2026)
  • WeChat 1.4B MAU, overseas Service Accounts: Omnichat, WeChat guide 2026
  • Consent friction scores: my own assessment (GDPR, UK GDPR/PECR, LGPD, POPIA, DPDP, PIPL), not legal advice.
